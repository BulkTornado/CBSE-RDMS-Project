I guess CBSE stores all exam results for an academic year in one database.
Might be named as CBSE_EXAM_RESULT_Y20XX for a particular year.
Each database will have 3 tables:
    1.  SUBJECT to store all subjects offered in that year.
    2.  SCHOOL_DATA to store all affiliated school for that year.
    3.  STUDENT_DATA to store all registered student's details for that year,
        along with their exam results. Secondary and Senior Secondary students are all mixed together.
        ->  Reasoning: I remember from the exam result login page that it only asked for year of examination and student's registration number.
